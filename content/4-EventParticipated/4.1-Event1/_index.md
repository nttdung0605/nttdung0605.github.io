# FCAJ Community Day - June 2026

### Event Summary
This event was a community-focused session that brought together speakers and attendees to discuss cloud careers, AI applications, and practical voice AI solutions. It provided an opportunity to learn from professionals and see how emerging technologies are being applied in real-world projects.

### Speakers
- **Steve Trần** — representative of Cloud Thinker / Cloud Hinker
- **Hiếu Nghị** — speaker on Voice AI topics
- **Kiệt** — presenter of a live Voice Agent demo
- **Trung** — Founder and CEO of R AI, sharing practical voice AI deployment experience

### Main Activities
- Opening remarks and career-sharing session by Steve Trần
- Presentation on Voice AI architecture and its applications
- Live demo of a Voice Agent system
- Discussion on production deployment challenges such as scalability, security, tool-calling, and auditing

### Key Highlights
- The event introduced the career journey of professionals working in cloud and AI fields.
- It explained the basic architecture of Voice AI systems, from speech-to-text to LLM processing and text-to-speech.
- The live demo showed how a voice assistant could interact with users in real time.
- The speakers emphasized the importance of production readiness, including latency, output control, auditing, and versioning.

### What I Learned
- Practical experience and early internships can strongly improve career opportunities.
- Cloud and AI skills are becoming increasingly important in modern technology careers.
- Voice AI requires not only model knowledge but also careful system design and production controls.
- Real-world AI systems must balance performance, reliability, security, and user experience.

### Takeaways for My Career
- Continue building practical cloud and AI skills.
- Strengthen understanding of production-level system design.
- Learn more about AI integration, automation, and responsible deployment.
- Stay active in community events to expand knowledge and professional networks.
