# AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Applications

### Event Summary
This event focused on how cloud computing is being applied in enterprise environments, especially in Vietnam. It highlighted the growing demand for cloud talent, the career path for students and early-career professionals, and real-world examples of cloud architecture in industries such as finance, retail, and manufacturing.

### Speakers
- **Quỳnh Mai** — MC / host
- **Nguyễn Trần Minh Duy** — Industry Liaison
- **Nguyễn Gia Hưng** — Head of Solution Architect, AWS Vietnam — main speaker
- **Student representatives** — asked questions and shared their study tour experiences
- **Representatives from Cloud Kinetics and Renova Cloud** — joined the discussion

### Main Activities
- Opening and introduction of the study tour program
- Main presentation on the cloud market in Vietnam and career opportunities
- Interactive Q&A session with students
- Discussion on industry trends, professional growth, and cloud adoption

### Key Highlights
- The cloud market in Vietnam is growing rapidly, especially with a Cloud First mindset.
- Employers increasingly value practical skills such as Kubernetes, cloud-native development, and AI-related knowledge.
- Certifications and public projects are important for building visibility and improving career opportunities.
- Networking and community participation play a strong role in professional growth.

### What I Learned
- The cloud industry is becoming more important for businesses and talent development.
- Practical skills and real-world experience matter more than theory alone.
- Building public projects and maintaining a visible professional profile can strengthen future job opportunities.
- Continuous learning and community engagement are essential for long-term career growth.

### Takeaways for My Career
- Focus on practical cloud and software engineering skills.
- Build public projects and improve portfolio quality.
- Stay active in professional communities and keep learning consistently.
- Prepare for a career that combines technical knowledge with business understanding.
