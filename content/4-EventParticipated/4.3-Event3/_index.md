# FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!

### Event Summary
This event was a fast-paced build competition where participants formed teams and developed AI-based prototypes within a limited time. The event focused on teamwork, rapid product development, pitching skills, and the practical application of AI and cloud technologies.

### Speakers and Participants
- **Nguyễn Gia Hưng** – Head of Solution Architect, AWS Vietnam
- **Joseph Marazota** – Head of Technology, AWS Asia
- **Student teams** — created ideas, built products, and presented their work
- **Judges and guests from the AWS Study Group** — evaluated projects and provided feedback

### Main Activities
1. **Opening session** — introduced the event, rules, and the spirit of “Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!”
2. **Team formation and idea development** — teams discussed ideas, assigned roles, and defined product scope
3. **Product development** — participants built prototypes, integrated AI and cloud tools, and prepared demos
4. **Pitching and demo presentation** — each team presented its product and answered questions from the judges
5. **Feedback and awards** — judges shared feedback and selected the winning team

### Key Highlights
- The event emphasized teamwork, creativity, and collaboration under time pressure.
- Participants built real AI prototypes and demonstrated how AI and cloud services can be applied in practice.
- The judging session provided valuable feedback that helped teams improve their ideas and products.

### Project Ideas Presented by the Teams

#### Team 1: Voice Commerce Assistant
- Idea: A voice-based assistant to support online shopping.
- Main activity: Demonstrated speech recognition in Vietnamese and product recommendation through AI.
- Highlight: Combined speech-to-text and LLM capabilities to provide product suggestions.

#### Team 2: AI Study Helper
- Idea: A study support tool for students that answers questions and summarizes materials.
- Main activity: Demonstrated a chatbot that explains concepts and generates quick quizzes.
- Highlight: Showed real-time interaction and improved learning efficiency.

#### Team 3: Customer Service Agent
- Idea: An AI agent for customer support in businesses.
- Main activity: Demonstrated responses to product questions and basic complaint handling.
- Highlight: Included a fallback mechanism to human staff when the AI could not handle the request.

#### Team 4: Healthcare Assistant
- Idea: An AI assistant for helping patients access health information and book appointments.
- Main activity: Demonstrated responses to common medical questions and appointment suggestions.
- Highlight: Highlighted the practical value of combining voice interaction with basic health data.

### What I Learned
- Teamwork, role distribution, and time management are essential for successful project execution.
- Pitching skills are important for presenting ideas clearly and persuasively.
- Building a prototype quickly requires strong problem definition, focus, and adaptation.

### Takeaways for My Career
- I learned how to turn ideas into working demos in a short period of time.
- I gained a better understanding of how AI and cloud technologies can solve real-world problems.
- I became more confident in presenting and defending ideas in front of an audience.


### Final Reflection
This event was a valuable experience because it showed how quickly ideas can become working AI prototypes when teams collaborate well. It also strengthened my understanding of product pitching, rapid development, and the practical use of AI in real-world applications.
