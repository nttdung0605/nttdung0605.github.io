### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment was friendly, professional, and supportive. I had the chance to learn in a positive atmosphere where questions were welcomed and collaboration was encouraged. The environment helped me focus better and feel more confident during the learning process.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor and admin team were very helpful throughout the internship. They provided clear guidance, shared useful knowledge, and created opportunities for me to practice independently. I especially appreciated that they did not only give answers, but also encouraged me to think and solve problems by myself.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The work was highly relevant to my academic background in information technology and software development. I was able to apply concepts from cloud computing, system design, and software development while also learning new topics such as serverless architecture, APIs, and modern AI applications.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This internship helped me improve many important skills, especially cloud architecture, API development, teamwork, communication, and problem-solving. I also gained more confidence in presenting ideas and learning from real-world examples shared during the workshops and events.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture was positive and professional. People respected each other, worked seriously, and still maintained a friendly atmosphere. This made me feel comfortable and motivated to contribute, even as an intern.

### Additional Questions

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
The most satisfying part was being able to complete a real project and see how the concepts I learned in the worklog and proposal could be applied in practice. I also enjoyed joining the events, where I learned about cloud careers, voice AI, and agentic AI in a very engaging way.

- **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
I think the company could provide even more structured onboarding at the beginning, along with more practical examples and guided project materials for interns.

- **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would recommend it. The program offers a strong learning environment, supports personal growth, and gives interns a chance to explore real cloud and AI technologies in a professional setting.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- I suggest keeping the internship experience practical and hands-on, while also providing more opportunities for interns to present their ideas and projects.
- I would like to continue being involved in similar programs in the future because they provide valuable exposure to the industry.
- Overall, this internship gave me a deeper understanding of cloud technology, teamwork, and professional growth, and I am grateful for the experience.
